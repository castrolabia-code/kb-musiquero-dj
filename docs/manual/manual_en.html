KB MUSIQUERO DJ

by Castro Labs · User manual

1. Getting started

  1. Activation: the first time, paste your product key (it starts with KBDJ1-) and click ACTIVATE. If your license is bound to a PC, send Castro Labs the PC ID shown in that window.
  2. Audio: in Options > Audio choose your sound card. For low latency use exclusive WASAPI (or ASIO if available) with a 128-256 sample buffer.
  3. Outputs: in Options > Mixer and outputs choose where the master goes (outputs 1-2 or 3-4) and the headphones. With a 2-output card, use SPLIT: master on the left and headphones on the right (mono).
  4. Music: drag songs or folders from Explorer onto the library (bottom), or click the cyan +. Tags and artwork are read, and BPM and key are analysed in the background.

2. Decks

Load a song by dragging it onto a deck, double-clicking it in the list (goes to the free deck) or right-click > Load on Deck 1/2.

ControlWhat it does
PLAY / PAUSEPlay / pause.
CUECDJ style: while playing returns to the cue point and pauses; paused sets the cue; held on the cue, previews.
SYNCMatches tempo and beats with the other deck and keeps them in phase.
TEMPO faderChanges speed (down = faster). The ±8% button switches to ±16% and ±50%.
KEY LOCK / KEY - / KEY +Keeps the pitch when changing tempo / shifts the key in semitones (double-click: original).
PlatterWith VINYL on, grab it with the mouse to scratch. Without VINYL, turn it to speed up or slow down briefly (pitch bend). The mouse wheel also bends.
SLIPAfter a loop, scratch or reverse, the song continues where it would have been.

The artwork spins in the middle of the platter and its orange ring pulses on every beat.

Right-click a pad for more options (delete cue, sample volume and mode, MIDI Learn). Hot cues are saved per song.

4. Mixer

GAIN, HIGH / MID / LOW EQ (fully down: kill), FILTER (left low-pass, right high-pass), volume faders and crossfader (curve and reverse in Options). MASTER has a safety limiter (LIMIT light). Channel CUE: listen in headphones; CUE MIX: cue/master blend in the headphones; PHONES: headphone volume.

AUTO MIX

Hands over smoothly to the other deck (starts it, syncs it, swaps the bass and moves the crossfader). The MIX knob sets the duration (2 to 60 s). Touching the crossfader cancels.

5. Effects

Two units (FX 1 and FX 2): effect, length in beats (BPM synced), parameter, WET and target 1 / 2 / M. Effects: Echo, Delay, Reverb, Flanger, Phaser, Filter, Bitcrusher, Gater, Roll, Brake, Backspin and Noise. Echo, delay and reverb ring out when turned off.

6. Library

  • All tracks, Preparation, History (what played this session) and your playlists (create with the green +; double-click to rename; right-click to delete).
  • Drag songs from the list onto a playlist or Preparation to add them.
  • Search: type parts of the title, artist, genre, BPM or key (Esc clears).
  • Columns: click to sort, drag to resize, right-click the header to show/hide. Rating: click the stars.
  • The Key column turns green when it matches what is playing.
  • Large library (LIBRARY button or Ctrl+B) shrinks the controller.

7. MIDI controllers

Plug the controller via USB: it is detected automatically. To map: right-click any control > MIDI Learn and move the physical control. Controller lights are handled automatically. Options > MIDI controllers lets you view, remove, import and export mappings.

9. Troubleshooting

  • Audio dropouts or clicks: raise the buffer size in Options > Audio, close other audio apps, use exclusive WASAPI.
  • No headphones: check output routing or enable SPLIT with a 2-output card.
  • M4A does not load on Windows N: install Microsoft's "Media Feature Pack".
  • Display glitches: turn off "Draw with the GPU" in Options > Preferences.
  • The program closed: on the next start it offers the error report; send it to Castro Labs support.

Your data (library, hot cues, playlists, MIDI mappings, license) lives in %APPDATA%\Castro Labs\KB Musiquero DJ. Uninstalling does not delete it.
